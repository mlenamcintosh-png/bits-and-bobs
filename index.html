Bits & Bobs is an online thrift shop in Barbados for clothes, accessories, home finds and everything in between. Some things are preloved, some are new—all have a little something to offer.

Our idea is simple: make finding your next favourite thing feel fun.

A few little answers

Is everything secondhand?

We sell both preloved and new items. Each listing will explain its condition, including any wear or flaws.

How do I place an order?

When items are available, choose one and tap its WhatsApp enquiry button. We’ll confirm availability and arrange the next steps with you.

How does collection or delivery work?

Please confirm the available options, location and any delivery charge with us before purchasing.

Found something you love?
